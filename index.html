Margin Golf

Mini golf, doodled in the margins.

A one-finger mini golf game drawn on notebook paper. Pull back, let go, and bank your ball round inked rails, past spinning pencils and sliding erasers, into the cup. The twist: red and blue pen gates swap every shot. 27 free holes in three courses, three new holes every day, no ads.

How to play

  • Putt: put a finger anywhere on the page, pull back away from where you want the ball to go, and let go. The further you pull, the harder the putt. Pull back to where you started to cancel.
  • Ink rails bounce the ball. Highlighter is sand and slows it down. Spilt ink costs a stroke and puts the ball back where you putted from.
  • Arrows on the paper are a slope; a belt of moving arrows carries the ball along. Punched holes come in pairs: in one, out of the other at the same speed.
  • Erasers slide and pencils spin on their own — time your putt, or bounce off them.
  • Pen gates: red ink is solid on your 1st, 3rd, 5th… shot; blue ink on the 2nd, 4th, 6th…. They swap every time the ball stops. The bar under the page tells you which one is solid now.
  • Each hole has a par. Take more than par + 4 strokes and you pick up. Finish nine holes for a medal: gold at par or better, silver within four, bronze for finishing.
  • The Daily Three is the same three holes for every player that day. One go; your card is saved hole by hole, and you can share your score as text.

Help

Can every hole really be done in par? Yes. Every hole is played through the game's own physics by a solver before release, and it finishes each one (and its mirror image) within par.

Balls and paper. New balls unlock as you finish courses. They change the look only; every ball rolls the same.

Paused? The game pauses when you leave the app or when the screen changes size mid-shot (for example when a foldable iPhone is folded or unfolded). Your round is saved after every hole.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

Refunds. Apple handles payments and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

Move or back up your progress. Everything lives only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup. There is no account or server to restore it from.
